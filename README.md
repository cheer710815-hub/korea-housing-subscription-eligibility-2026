# Korea housing subscription: public-sale vs. private-sale quick check (2026)

An independent, source-linked checklist for readers comparing general-supply eligibility for public-sale (국민주택) and private-sale (민영주택) housing in South Korea. This is a navigation aid, not a personal eligibility determination. The applicable recruitment notice and current law control.

## Start with the notice

Before checking account duration or household status, identify the exact housing type, supply category (general or special), recruitment-notice date, residence area, and whether the notice is an original or corrected version. The same project may use different rules by supply category and unit type.

## General-supply comparison

| Checkpoint | Public-sale / 국민주택 | Private-sale / 민영주택 |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription account | In general, a housing subscription savings account is required by the recruitment-notice date; check exceptions in the rule and notice. | In general, a housing subscription savings account is required by the recruitment-notice date; check exceptions in the rule and notice. |
| First-priority test | The rule uses account subscription period and recognized monthly payment count; region and notice-specific extensions may apply. | The rule uses account subscription period and the required deposit balance; region and restricted-area conditions may add requirements. |
| How competition is resolved | For ordinary general supply, the applicable priority sequence and recognized payment record matter; do not substitute the private-sale score table. | Depending on size, location, and notice, competition may use points, lottery, or a prescribed mix. A first-priority label alone does not determine winning chances. |
| Household / home ownership | Check the specific supply type and notice; public-sale programs often require household-level no-home status, but do not generalize special-supply rules to every route. | Restrictions can depend on regulated area, supply type, housing size, and selection method. Check the notice rather than assuming all owned homes disqualify. |
| Final authority | Housing Supply Rule Article 27 and the individual recruitment notice. | Housing Supply Rule Article 28 and the individual recruitment notice. |

## Reader's pre-application checklist

1. Save the latest recruitment notice and every correction/attachment; compare notice dates and correction history.
2. Confirm whether the home is 국민주택 or 민영주택, and whether the application is general or special supply.
3. Check the applicant's residence, household composition, home-ownership history, and any household member restrictions as of the relevant notice date.
4. For 국민주택, verify the recognized payment count and sequence in the bank record; for 민영주택, verify the deposit amount for the intended area and unit size.
5. Check restricted-area requirements, re-winning limits, resale/occupancy conditions, and supply-specific exceptions in the notice.
6. Recheck the application calendar and entered answers in 청약Home or the named official application channel before final submission.
